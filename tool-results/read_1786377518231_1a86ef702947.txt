    28→### Task ID: 20-2 — Fix Screen Movement/Jitter (Up/Down/Left/Right)
    29→**Status**: ✅ Completed
    30→**Priority**: Critical (UX-breaking)
    31→
    32→**Problem**: The webapp's screen moved up and down, left and right — causing jittering/shaking during interaction and scrolling.
    33→
    34→**Root Causes Identified**:
    35→1. **No `overflow-x: hidden`** on `html`/`body` — content could cause horizontal scrollbar
    36→2. **No `overscroll-behavior`** — browser rubber-banding caused unexpected viewport shifts
    37→3. **Global `transition` on ALL elements** (`*, *::before, *::after`) — caused layout thrashing on every render
    38→4. **`Map` icon from lucide-react shadowing JS built-in** in 4 additional files (explore-tab, tools-tab, questionnaire-tab, dialogs)
    39→
    40→**Fixes Applied**:
    41→- **globals.css line 129-138**: Added `overflow-x: hidden` and `overscroll-behavior: none` to `html`; `overflow-x: hidden` and `overscroll-behavior-y: contain` to `body`
    42→- **globals.css line 141-149**: Replaced global `*, *::before, *::after` transition with targeted selector list (`a, button, input, select, .card`, etc.) to eliminate layout thrashing
    43→- **page.tsx line 186**: Added `overflow-x-hidden` to root container, removed `scroll-smooth` (moved to CSS layer)
    44→- **4 files renamed `Map` → `MapIcon`**: explore-tab.tsx (4 usages), tools-tab.tsx (2 usages), questionnaire-tab.tsx (import only), dialogs.tsx (2 usages)
    45→

    65→- **shared-components-2.tsx**: Removed `scroll-smooth` from Featured Destinations carousel; added `overflow-hidden` to section wrapper
    66→- **shared-components-3.tsx**: Removed `-mx-4 px-4` negative margins from stat cards carousel; removed `scroll-smooth` from alert banner
